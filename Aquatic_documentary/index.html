Interview

Jason
Carrela

What drew you to deep-water documentation?
Most people will never experience the kind of silence that exists 200 meters down. The pressure becomes something physical — you feel it in your chest, in your ears. I wanted to bring that feeling to the surface. Film was the only way I knew how.
How has the reef changed since your first dive there in 2009?
The bleaching events of 2016 and 2020 were devastating to witness. But there are pockets of incredible recovery. Species returning to areas we thought were lost. The ocean is resilient in ways that still surprise me after thirty years in the water…
